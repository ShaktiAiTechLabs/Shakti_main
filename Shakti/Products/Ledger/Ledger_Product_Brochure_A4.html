Ledger

Every invoice read, checked against its purchase order, and given a verdict — with a record of who changed what.

Ledger ingests a vendor invoice, runs OCR, extracts every field with a confidence score, links the purchase order automatically, then scores the match point by point. Finance sees a pass, a review flag, or a rejection — and the reasoning behind it.

Invoice review — extracted fields highlighted on the source document, verified values alongside

Reads the document

Text-layer PDFs and scanned images, processed in-house. Every field carries its own confidence score.

Finds the PO itself

The PO number read off the invoice is looked up and linked automatically — no manual selection.

Scores the match

Vendor, totals, currency, line items and approval state compared point by point, discrepancies itemised.

Proves the trail

Every correction logged old value to new, with author and timestamp. Passed invoices lock.

How it works

1

Upload

Drag in a PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB.

2

Extract

OCR reads the document; fields come back scored.

3

Review

A person checks values against the original and corrects.

4

Match

Point-scored against the linked PO; duplicates caught.

5

Verdict

Pass, review or reject — the PO closes on a pass.

Inside the product

Capabilities

Processing

  • In-process OCR — no external API
  • Per-field confidence scores
  • Inline correction with change history
  • Duplicate detection by file hash
  • Anomaly risk scoring

Vendors & POs

  • Per-vendor required fields
  • Vendor spend and flag history
  • Server-computed PO totals
  • Line-item similarity matching
  • CSV export honouring filters

Access

  • Admin, Accountant, Viewer roles
  • Enforced server-side
  • JWT auth, self-serve reset
  • Admin team management
  • Per-invoice action log

Operations

  • Background queue for OCR
  • Clustered across CPU cores
  • Self-hosted object storage
  • Rate limiting per IP
  • Degrades gracefully at every layer
